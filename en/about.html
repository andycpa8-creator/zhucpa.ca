About Andy Zhu

Andy Zhu, CPA, LPA, Master’s degree in Accounting, Master of Accountancy, Chartered Professional Accountant, Licensed Public Accountant. He graduated from Goodman School of Business, Brock University, Canada. He has many years of professional work experience in accounting, auditing and taxation in CPA firms, including KPMG LLP and Toronto medium-sized accounting firms. Andy Zhu is in contact with many types of industries, mainly including Real Estate, Pharmaceutical, Technology, Financial Services and Trade. He is familiar with the personal tax, corporate tax and financial statements of these industries. He also had audit experience with government agencies and large Canadian listed companies. A fluent Mandarin speaker, Andy Zhu can provide professional Mandarin services to clients.
